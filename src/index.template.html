    Photosensitivity notice

    Ashfall Excavation contains flashing lights, lightning, muzzle flashes, explosions and sudden changes in brightness. For a small number of people, patterns of light like these can trigger seizures, even if they have never had one before and have no history of epilepsy.

    If you feel dizzy, notice twitching or blurred vision, lose awareness of your surroundings or feel unwell in any way while playing, stop at once and speak to a doctor before you play again.

    Play in a well lit room, sit back from the screen and rest for a few minutes every hour.

    EYES ONLY

    The dig crew struck something black and humming under the mud. Then the dead started climbing out of the trenches.

    Hold out round after round. Board the windows, open the site, restore the power, and forge your guns into something worse. Finish the old rite the diggers started: ride the Colossus that stalks No Man's Land, read the chapel's glass, build a storm in your fist, and bring the giant down. Somewhere under the dig is a way out.

    Rumors from the line

    The relic crate sometimes coughs up weapons nobody issued: a pistol that spits green fire, a rifle that throws lightning from one corpse to the next. The quartermaster sells wind-up drummers the dead cannot resist.

      Before your first night

      Whoever finds this: the last crew left in a hurry. Here is what kept me breathing.

      1. Windows first. Hold E at a broken window to nail the boards back. The dead come through the gaps.
      2. Points buy everything. Hits and kills pay. Spend them on doors, wall guns, tonics and the relic crate.
      3. Find the generator. Throw its lever and the tonic machines and the Forge wake up.
      4. Aim high. Headshots pay more and end fights faster. The knife F lunges when you run dry.
      5. Read this journal. Its corner of the screen lists what the dig is hiding. Fold it with J.

      Scrawled in the margin: the bell, the candles and the chapel glass are not decoration. Neither is the giant.
